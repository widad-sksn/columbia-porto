Pemasangan WiFi

Plumbing

DevOps

Setting MikroTik

Bandwidth management, captive portal hotspot voucher, multi-WAN load balancing & failover, firewall protection, dan VPN.

Service Computer

Perbaikan PC desktop & laptop, install ulang OS, upgrade SSD/RAM, maintenance hardware rutin, dan pemulihan data.

Pemasangan Penangkal Petir
